# Legal Information

## About this fork

Kids MDM IM is a downstream fork of [Molly](https://github.com/mollyim/mollyim-android),
itself a hardened fork of Signal. It adds parental-control features (a
local conversation journal for the device owner, GIF removal, a settings PIN
lock, and per-type call blocking) intended for use with a parent's own
child-MDM deployment.

Kids MDM IM is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed
by, or sponsored by Molly, Signal Messenger, LLC, or the Signal Foundation.
"Molly" and "Signal" are referenced here solely for attribution and technical
identification of the upstream projects this fork is built on.

This fork intentionally changes the privacy properties Signal/Molly advertise
for the account holder's own copy of a conversation (e.g. disappearing
messages and delete-for-everyone are journaled locally before they take
effect). It should not be mistaken for unmodified Molly or Signal.
